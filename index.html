    An architecture without batch statistics

    Weight standardization normalizes each layer’s weights instead of its activations, so the architecture never pools batch statistics that differ from one client to the next.

    Federated learning with differential privacy, 3.4 points lost. Group normalization loses 17.5 at the same budget.

    Inside the architecture

    Every block standardizes its weights, then a channel attention module rescales its feature maps. Attention learns to raise the channels that stay stable across clients and to damp the ones that shift.

    Federated aggregation methods

    Works with FedAvg, FedProx, FedAdam and SCAFFOLD, and with FedPer for personalized learning.

    ImageNet weights for the architecture

    Checkpoints with SE, ECA and CBAM attention, at 80.4 to 80.61 top-1.

    Architecture overhead

    About 10% more time per training step than a batch-normalized ResNet, and less memory.

    Questions about ANFR

    Which aggregation methods does the architecture work with?

    Any. The paper tests FedAvg, FedProx, FedAdam and SCAFFOLD, and FedPer for personalized learning, where ANFR scores highest on both benchmarks.

    Why does the architecture drop batch normalization?

    Batch normalization needs large batches and does not work with differential privacy. Group normalization, its usual stand-in, does not reliably beat it.

    How do I load the weights in my code?

    Call timm.create_model with the Hub id and pretrained=True. For the CBAM variant, import fl_bench/networks/anfr.py first.
